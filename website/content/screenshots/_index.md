## Translations

The JavaFX interface in each of its 20 bundled languages. It follows the operating-system language by default; pick another one under _File > Preferences > General > Language_. Translations are maintained on [Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/projects/jsignpdf/messages/) — help is welcome.
